What approval means in this demo

The first 25 observations form the reference proposal. Review their suitability, then record a reason to create the next baseline revision.

Correcting a reference observation invalidates the active approval. Collection and specification checks continue while statistical monitoring awaits review.

Role switching demonstrates the workflow. This prototype has no production identity or authorization service.
