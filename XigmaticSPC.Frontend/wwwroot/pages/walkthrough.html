Opening: “We will follow one order through measurement, action and evidence. These are illustrative values for a synthetic packaging process.”

Net weight493 gOutside the demo range 495–505 g
Seal width4.00 mmWithin the demo range 3.50–4.50 mm
Label position10.00 mmWithin the demo range 8.00–12.00 mm

Response example: Suspected cause: “Filler setting drift.” Containment: “Affected trays held for review.” Action: “Filler adjusted; follow-up checks requested.” Assign an owner and a future deadline, add a note and acknowledge. Complete the inspection; the event remains open.

Follow-up: Record 500 g, then another 500 g. The first recovery can trigger a moving-range signal because the change from 493 g is large. Record its response too. The second 500 g is the clean follow-up you can link when closing both investigations as Quality engineer.

Finish: Open the order report, show the original deviation and verification, then export the measurements or evidence package. “This is the record your team can follow from order to outcome.”
